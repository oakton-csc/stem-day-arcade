Binary Blitz

Computers store every number as bits: switches that are on (1) or off (0). Each switch is worth a power of two. Turn on the right switches so they add up to the big number.

Click a switch or press 1–8. Enter skips a number but costs 3 seconds. You have 60 seconds.
