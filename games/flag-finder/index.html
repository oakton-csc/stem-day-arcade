Flag Finder

    By Oakton CSC
    
Somewhere inside a mountain is one block that doesn’t belong: the flag. Checking every block one at a time takes forever. This trick finds it in a handful of tests.

    
How to play: click a block to hide the flag (or press R), then press Run the finder and watch. It copies every normal block onto a slab of gold, folds the leftover gold into one corner, and reads the flag’s position in binary.
